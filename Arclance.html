Introduction to ArcLance
ArcLance is a Web3 payment platform built specifically for freelancers in countries where traditional payment services like PayPal, Payoneer, and Stripe are restricted or unavailable — including Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nigeria, and 150+ other countries.
⚡

Payments confirm instantly on Arc Network — no bank delays

🔒
Self-Custodial

What Problem Does ArcLance Solve?
Millions of talented freelancers in South Asia and Africa cannot receive payments from international clients because:

✗ PayPal is unavailable or heavily restricted in Pakistan, Bangladesh, and many African countries
✗ Payoneer charges 2–3% fees and requires lengthy KYC verification
✗ Wire transfers (SWIFT) take 3–5 days and cost $25–$45 per transaction
✗ Fiverr/Upwork take 20% platform fees and hold funds for 14 days

ArcLance solves this by using USDC on Arc Network — a blockchain specifically designed for stablecoin payments, with near-zero fees and 2-second finality.
Currently on Testnet: ArcLance runs on Arc Testnet. All USDC used is test USDC with no real monetary value. This allows you to explore every feature safely before the mainnet launch.

Payment Flow — What Happens When You Send USDC
①You enter recipient address and amount in the Send form
②ArcLance calls the USDC smart contract's transfer(to, amount) function
③Your wallet (MetaMask/Rabby) signs the transaction — you approve it
④The signed transaction is broadcast to Arc Testnet nodes
⑤Arc Network confirms the block in ~2 seconds — USDC arrives in recipient's wallet
✓Transaction hash recorded on-chain, viewable at testnet.arcscan.app
Why Arc Network?
Arc Network is a stablecoin-native EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain built and maintained by the Arc team. Unlike Ethereum (where gas is paid in ETH) or other L2s (where you need the native token), Arc pays all gas fees in USDC — making it ideal for freelancers who only hold stablecoins and don't want to buy volatile crypto tokens just to pay transaction fees.

A Pay Link is a shareable URL that contains your wallet address, requested amount, and service label. When a client opens it, they see a payment page where they can connect their wallet and pay you — without needing to know your 0x address.

Bridge Assets
Move USDC between Arc Testnet and other blockchains using Circle's CCTP protocol.
What is CCTP?
Circle Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP) is Circle's official bridge for moving USDC between blockchains. It works by burning USDC on the source chain and minting fresh USDC on the destination — no wrapped tokens, no custodial risk. This is the most secure way to bridge USDC.
Supported Routes
Arc Testnet ↔ Ethereum Sepolia~15 min

FAQ
Frequently asked questions about ArcLance.
Do I need ETH to pay gas fees?
No. Arc Network's gas is paid in USDC — you never need to buy ETH or any other volatile token.
Is this real money?
ArcLance currently runs on Arc Testnet. All USDC is test USDC with no real value. Mainnet is planned for a future release.
What is USDC?
USDC is a regulated stablecoin issued by Circle (US-regulated). 1 USDC = $1.00 USD always. Backed by cash and US Treasuries, audited monthly by Deloitte.
My client doesn't know crypto. Can they pay me?
Yes — they'll need MetaMask and USDC (one-time setup, ~15 minutes). Share the AI Assistant with them for guidance. Future versions will include a dedicated client onboarding flow.
Swap vs Bridge — what's the difference?
Swap = exchange token A for token B on the same chain (USDC → EURC on Arc). Bridge = move token across chains (USDC from Arc → Ethereum).
Can ArcLance freeze my funds?
No. ArcLance is self-custodial — your money stays in your wallet. We never hold, access, or control your funds. Only your private key (in your wallet) can authorize transactions.
Is my wallet address public?
Yes — all blockchain transactions are publicly viewable on the block explorer (testnet.arcscan.app). This is normal for blockchains. Your name is not attached to your address unless you voluntarily share both.
